Who this is for

US firms that take injury or med mal cases and aren't showing up in their own cities. Usually one of these:

  • Solo lawyers. Most of your cases come from referrals. Google sends you close to nothing, and you've stopped expecting it to.
  • Small firms, 2 to 10 lawyers. You've paid for a nice website, maybe an agency too. Still, search "truck accident lawyer" plus your city and you're on page two.
  • Multi-office firms. You rank fine where the main office is. In the other cities where you have offices, you basically don't exist.

What I actually do

Rankings are only worth something if the phone rings. So the work comes in two parts: get you to the top, then turn those visits into consultations.

Getting you to the top

  • Practice pages for the cases you take. A proper medical malpractice page, then birth injury, surgical errors, misdiagnosis, truck accidents, whatever you actually handle. Not one line on a "Practice Areas" list.
  • City pages. One for each place you take cases from, with real local detail. Not the same page fifty times with the city name swapped.
  • Google Business Profile. Right categories, services filled in, photos, a simple way to ask clients for reviews, regular posts. This is what gets you into the map results.
  • Titles, headings and schema. So Google knows which page is for which search. Attorney and LegalService markup, done properly.
  • Citations. Your name, address and phone the same everywhere. Sounds small. It's often a mess.
  • Local links. Local news, bar associations, sponsorships, community sites in your area. Real sites, not link farms that get you in trouble later.

Turning searches into booked consultations

  • Pages that make people call. Click-to-call at the top on mobile, a clear free case review offer, and a short form. Someone who just got hurt won't fill in twelve fields.
  • Reviews that win the click. In the map, the firm with more recent reviews usually gets the call. I set up a simple routine so happy clients actually leave them.
  • Call and lead tracking. Tracked numbers and form tracking, so you can see how many calls and consultation requests came from Google, and from which city.
  • A monthly report you can read in two minutes. Calls, consultation requests, where you rank, and what I'm doing next.

How it works

  1. I look at where you stand

    Before our call I check how you rank for your main case types in your cities, what your Google profile looks like, and who's beating you. You get this even if we don't end up working together.

  2. I fix the pages and your Google profile

    The first month or two is the heavy part. Missing practice pages, thin city pages, titles, schema, profile cleanup.

  3. Then we keep building

    Every month after that: more city pages, more local links, more reviews coming in. And I track every call and form, so you see consultations, not just rankings.

What I won't promise

I won't promise you the number one spot or a set number of cases. Nobody can. Anyone who does is either guessing or buying links that will hurt you later.

What I will do is go after the top three in the map and page one for the searches that bring you cases, and show you every month the calls and bookings that came from it. In a competitive city, expect a few months before it shows up as calls. Sometimes longer. That's just how local search for lawyers works.
