The med mal directory

I built and run medicalmalpracticelawyernearme.com. It helps people find a medical malpractice lawyer in their city.

I built it to understand this niche from the inside. What people actually type after a surgery goes wrong. Which cities have real demand. And why directories beat firm websites so often.

The answer to that last one is pretty simple. A directory has a page for every city and every case type. Most firms have a homepage and one "Medical Malpractice" page. Google picks the page that matches the search, so the directory wins.

Problems I see all the time

These aren't client stories. They're the patterns I find when I look at PI and med mal firm sites. There's a good chance one of them is yours.

You take med mal cases, but there's no med mal page

What happens

It's one line on a "Practice Areas" list. Someone searches "medical malpractice lawyer" plus your city, and Google shows directories and a bigger firm, because they have a page about exactly that.

What I'd fix

A real med mal page, then pages for the case types you take: birth injury, surgical errors, misdiagnosis, medication errors. Written so a patient's family can follow it, and linked properly from the rest of the site.

One office, five cities, and the site only talks about one

What happens

You take cases from the whole area, but your site only mentions the city your office is in. In the other four, you're invisible.

What I'd fix

A page for each city you actually serve, with real local detail: the courthouse, the main hospitals, the roads where crashes happen. Service areas set in your Google profile. Not fifty copy-paste pages.

Your Google Business Profile was set up once and forgotten

What happens

Category is just "Lawyer", a handful of old reviews, no services listed. You don't show in the map results, and that's where a lot of injury calls come from.

What I'd fix

Primary category set to "Personal injury attorney", services and photos filled in, a simple routine for asking happy clients for reviews, and regular posts so the profile looks alive.
